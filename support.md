# Support

Thanks for using Ans or Ans Graph. Support is handled personally — you'll be
talking to the person who built the apps.

## Contact

Email: [support@anscalc.com](mailto:support@anscalc.com)

Include which app you are using and its version if you can. Replies usually
come within a few days.

## Reporting a bug

The most useful bug reports include:

1. which app you were using and what you entered or did (the exact key
   presses or graphing steps, if possible);
2. what you expected to see;
3. what the app showed instead;
4. your device and operating system version.

Calculation and graphing bugs are taken particularly seriously — if either
app ever shows a wrong answer, please report it.

## Feature requests

Suggestions are always welcome. The apps deliberately grow slowly — features
are added when they make them better to use every day, not to fill a list —
but every suggestion is read and considered.

## Frequently asked

**Do Ans and Ans Graph work offline?** Yes, completely. Neither app uses the
network.

**Does Ans Graph have accounts, ads or analytics?** No. It has no accounts,
ads, tracking, analytics SDK or third-party dependencies. Nothing leaves your
device.

**Where is my data?** On your device, and nowhere else. Ans Graph keeps saved
graphs locally; its widget reads a small file from the app's own private App
Group container and sends nothing. See [Privacy](/privacy).

**Is there a subscription?** No. Ans and Ans Graph are separate products;
each is bought once and owned.
