# Release Notes

The permanent changelog for Ans. The newest version is listed first.

## 1.2

- **Large SHIFT and VAR Labels**: a new option that moves each key's second
  function onto the key itself, at full size, while that mode is on —
  instead of leaving it in the small label above the key. Press SHIFT and
  the orange functions move onto their keys; press VAR and the teal variable
  letters do the same. The key's usual function takes its place in that
  small label, so nothing goes unnamed.
- Settings now gathers this and Large Display Text under an **Accessibility**
  heading, on both iPhone/iPad and Mac. On the Mac they are in the View menu
  as well.
- Both are off by default, and neither changes how the calculator works or
  how quickly it responds.

## 1.1

- **Ans came to Mac** — a real desk calculator in a compact window that can
  keep itself in front of the document you are working in. One purchase
  covers iPhone, iPad and Mac; there is nothing further to buy.
- On the Mac: type calculations straight from the keyboard, hold ⇧ while
  clicking a key for its shifted function and ⌥ for its variable, paste a
  whole expression with ⌘V, and open all six variables in their own window
  with ⇧⌘V.
- **Factorials**, on SHIFT ) — so 8!÷(3!×5!) is one line of keys. `!` also
  works from a hardware keyboard and in pasted expressions.
- Key presses now respond with feedback matched to what the key does.

## 1.0

*Initial release.*

- A full scientific calculator with the feel of a dedicated device: real
  keys, a two-line display, and instant response.
- Exact mathematics — fractions stay fractions, and π is kept exact, so
  ⅓ × 3 is exactly 1.
- Complete function set: trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots of any
  degree, powers of ten and random numbers.
- Six variables with names, a variable editor, and independent memory keys.
- Replay earlier calculations and edit any expression in place.
- Hardware keyboard support on iPad.
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep the latest answer close; Home Screen
  widgets can page through recent completed calculations
  with calculator-style ▲ and ▼ keys without opening Ans.
- A Control Centre control opens Ans in one tap.
- No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking — and it works entirely offline.
