Definitions

  • The degree divides a full turn into 360, a Babylonian convention that survives because 360 divides so many ways.
  • The radian is the SI unit of angle: the angle subtending an arc equal to the radius. A full turn is 2π radians.
  • This conversion is exact by definition. 1 ° = 0.01745329252 rad.
  • Angle is converted through rad.

How it works

One direction multiplies and the other divides by the same number, so nothing is lost by going back and forth.

The value passes through rad, the unit every other angle unit on the site is defined against, rather than a number written into this page on its own. That is why the figure here, the figure in the table above and the general angle converter always agree.

Display precision sets a maximum number of significant figures and trims trailing zeroes, so it changes what you see and never what was calculated. The editable box always holds a plain number at full working precision, so you can carry on from either side.

Common questions this answers: how many radians in a degree (0.0174533), how many degrees in a radian (57.2958), and what 90 ° is in radians (1.5708 rad).
