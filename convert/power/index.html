Definitions

  • Mechanical horsepower is 745.6999 W, defined as 550 foot-pounds per second. It is the horsepower UK and US brochures quote.
  • The kilowatt is 1000 watts. EU vehicle documents give engine power in kW, whatever the brochure says.
  • Every unit here is defined against the W.
  • 12 of the 13 units are exact by definition; the rest are conventional or measured values. The display says which kind you are looking at.

How it works

Every value is converted into watts and then back out into the unit you asked for. One route covers every pairing in the menus, so an unusual combination is no less accurate than a common one, and adding a unit affects only that unit.

Both boxes are live. Whichever one you last typed in is the source, changing either menu recalculates straight away, and Swap exchanges the units and the values so the number you are reading stays where you are looking.

Display precision limits significant figures rather than decimal places, because one fixed number of decimals is useless across a range this wide. Very large and very small results switch to scientific notation with a proper superscript exponent. The editable box always holds a plain number at full working precision, so you can carry on from either side.
