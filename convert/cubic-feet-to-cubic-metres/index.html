Definitions

  • The cubic foot is 28.3168 litres. UK gas meters installed before the 1990s still read in hundreds of cubic feet.
  • The cubic metre is the SI unit of volume, and holds exactly 1000 litres. Water and gas meters read in it.
  • This conversion is exact by definition. 1 ft³ = 0.02831684659 m³.
  • US and imperial units of the same name are different sizes. A US pint is 473 mL; an imperial pint is 568 mL. Every unit below says which system it belongs to.

How it works

One direction multiplies and the other divides by the same number, so nothing is lost by going back and forth.

The value passes through m³, the unit every other volume & capacity unit on the site is defined against, rather than a number written into this page on its own. That is why the figure here, the figure in the table above and the general volume & capacity converter always agree.

Display precision sets a maximum number of significant figures and trims trailing zeroes, so it changes what you see and never what was calculated. The editable box always holds a plain number at full working precision, so you can carry on from either side.
