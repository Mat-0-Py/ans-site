Definitions

  • The pound per square inch is exactly 6894.757 293 Pa. UK and US tyre gauges are still marked in it.
  • The bar is exactly 100 000 Pa, near enough to atmospheric pressure at sea level to be a convenient working unit.
  • This conversion is exact by definition. 1 psi = 0.06894757293 bar.
  • Pressure is converted through Pa.

How it works

One direction multiplies and the other divides by the same number, so nothing is lost by going back and forth.

The value passes through Pa, the unit every other pressure unit on the site is defined against, rather than a number written into this page on its own. That is why the figure here, the figure in the table above and the general pressure converter always agree.

Display precision sets a maximum number of significant figures and trims trailing zeroes, so it changes what you see and never what was calculated. The editable box always holds a plain number at full working precision, so you can carry on from either side.

Common questions this answers: how many bars in a pound per square inch (0.0689476), how many pounds per square inch in a bar (14.5038), and what 32 psi is in bars (2.20632 bar).
