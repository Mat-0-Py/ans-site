Constants in the language

Fifteen CODATA 2022 physical constants are tokens in the expression language, not numbers you look up and paste. h is the Planck constant everywhere an expression can go, each one carrying its unit and an Exact, Derived or Measured badge — so E = h × f plots where it actually lives, at 10⁻³³.
