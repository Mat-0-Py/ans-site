A plotted line is the start, not the result. Ans Graph gives the important points a value, a place and an honest statement of what was found.

Roots and intersections

Gradients

Choose a point and read the local slope with its tangent, so the number stays attached to the shape.

Trace and value table

Move along a curve for live coordinates, or compare functions at exact x-values in a table you can export.

Constants in the language

Fifteen CODATA 2022 physical constants are tokens in the expression language, not numbers you look up and paste. h is the Planck constant everywhere an expression can go, each one carrying its unit and an Exact, Derived or Measured badge — so E = h × f plots where it actually lives, at 10⁻³³.

Readable without colour

Every curve carries its own line pattern and token as well as its colour, so four functions on one graph stay four things when colour cannot carry them.

Take it with you

Export the plot as an image or a PDF, or the value table as CSV, straight to the files and apps you already use — from the device itself.

Deliberately focused

Fewer features. Each one excellent.

Ans Graph is not trying to win a feature-count contest. It concentrates on plotting, exploring and the analysis you need to trust, with numerical approximation treated as a result to explain rather than hide.

The shade, the handles, the value and its uncertainty are one idea — kept together on purpose.

One universal purchase

Your graphs, on the screen in front of you.

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Each layout is native to its screen, from a graph you can hold to a full desktop instrument.

Owned, not rented

The maths stays here.

Ans Graph works fully offline. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics, trackers or third-party dependencies. Saved graphs stay on your device, and the widget reads only a small local file in the app's own private container. Nothing is sent anywhere. Read the privacy promise.
